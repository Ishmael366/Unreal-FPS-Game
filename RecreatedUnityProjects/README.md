# Unity recreation studies

Twelve independently openable Unity 2021.3 LTS study projects, newly implemented from tutorial topics and transcript references. These are source prototypes, not recovered original projects or verified playable releases. Each includes its own scene, procedural art, controls, game rules, license, and provenance record.

- [Forest Survival](Forest-Survival/README.md) — Health, thirst, hunger and stamina, Resource pickups and survival clock, Camp objective and loss/restart loop
- [Wind Meadow](Wind-Meadow/README.md) — Rolling terrain and collision, 25 mesh grass patches with a custom wind shader, Grass visibility, wind and sun controls
- [LAN Arena](LAN-Arena/README.md) — Host and join through real UDP sockets, Host-authoritative movement and snapshots, Eight-player cap, bounded packets and disconnect cleanup
- [Shrine Path](Shrine-Path/README.md) — Click-to-move with world collision, Selected-target approach and melee combat, Four enemies, healing pickup and shrine objective
- [Sunlit Side Runner](Sunlit-Side-Runner/README.md) — Side-view movement and gap jumping, Stomp defeats, bonus blocks and token pickups, Original geometric art; no Nintendo assets
- [Castle Relics](Castle-Relics/README.md) — Double-jump and dash unlocks, Melee combat, sealed gate and boss encounter, Side-view castle route and healing pickup
- [Neon Vault](Neon-Vault/README.md) — Top-down arena and encounter activation, Pistol, spread and automatic fire modes, Pursuers, ranged enemy, volley boss and exit gate
- [Silent Extraction](Silent-Extraction/README.md) — Third-person camera and hitscan weapons, Guard and drone enemies with health, Key, locked gate, terminal, two generators and extraction
- [Sky Island Trail](Sky-Island-Trail/README.md) — Third-person platforming over twelve islands, Moving platform carry and gem collection, Fall loss, beacon requirement and restart
- [Archive Escape](Archive-Escape/README.md) — Two fixed camera regions and shoulder-camera toggle, Key-locked door and escape mechanism, Slow pursuers, limited ammunition and flashlight
- [Facility Breach](Facility-Breach/README.md) — First-person shooting, ammunition and reload timing, Patrolling ranged enemies and line of sight, Cover layout, supplies and extraction objective
- [Shield Arena](Shield-Arena/README.md) — Pursuit and melee attack timing, Directional shield mitigation and stamina, Two health bars, victory/defeat and restart

## Validation

All twelve variants compile against Unity 2021.3 reference assemblies using C# 7.3. The standalone rule and UDP loopback checks pass 39 assertions. Unity editor import, shader compilation, runtime balancing and export have not been verified; demos are deferred. See VALIDATION.json.

Open an individual project folder in Unity, open Assets/Scenes/Main.unity, then press Play and Start. Levels are generated at runtime. The LAN project uses native desktop UDP and does not implement WebGL networking.

Art and sound are newly generated by the implementation. Third-party transcripts, paid assets, and original creator project files are not included. Per-project SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json records the design reference.
